Mapling opens your Freeplane mind maps (.mm files) on your phone or tablet, so that you can read them and make quick changes anywhere: on the bus before a meeting, away from your computer.

Need a new map? Create a simple one in seconds: a few taps add its branches, and it opens in Freeplane like any other.

Open a map from Google Drive or any other storage, change it, and save it back to the same file. Mapling only changes what you edit: notes, icons, styles, links and everything else Freeplane wrote are kept exactly as they were, so the map opens in Freeplane as before.

What you can do:
- open a map from Google Drive, the phone's storage or any document provider;
- create a simple new map in seconds;
- edit the text of a node, add, delete and move nodes;
- undo and redo;
- zoom with two fingers, move the selected node by holding it, fold and unfold branches;
- reopen your recent maps in one gesture; Mapling reopens the map you left open.

Your work is safe:
- unsaved changes are kept as a draft, even if Android closes the app;
- before saving, Mapling checks that the map was not changed elsewhere (on your computer, for instance); if it was, you can save a copy and keep both versions;
- content Mapling does not display (notes, icons, attributes…) is never lost, and you are told when a map holds some.

Good to know: maps written by Freeplane 1.12 can be edited; older ones (FreeMind included) and newer ones open read-only. Formatted (rich) and multi-line node texts are shown, but can only be edited in Freeplane.

Private by design: no account, no ads, no tracking, and no network access at all. Mapling asks for no permission: it reads and writes only the maps you choose.
